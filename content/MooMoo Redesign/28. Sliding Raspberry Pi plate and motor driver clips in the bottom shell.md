# Goals
- Give the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W a printed mount inside the bottom shell
- Make the Pi removable without reaching into a shell that is 40 mm deep and full of actuators
- Hold the three TB6612FNG motor driver boards without screws
- Keep the six sockets, the snap fit and the outside of the shell unchanged

# Description
The first workbook ended with the electronics in a second, smaller casing beside the device because they did not fit inside (see [[18. Additional Compartment for Electronics.]]). In the redesign the electronics move into the bottom shell, between and around the six actuator sockets. The Raspberry Pi stands upright against one long wall and the three motor driver boards stand upright against the opposite long wall.

The Pi mount went through two forms. Until 9 May 2026 it was four round posts printed onto the wall, with screw holes of 1.8 mm. From 13 May it became a separate plate that carries the Pi and slides into two rails on the wall, where it clicks into place. The plate is called the slider in the source file. This step documents both forms and the clips for the driver boards.

Reading the dates back, the sliding plate was drawn in the four days after the whole actuator had gone onto one print plate (step 27, 12 May). The order makes sense. Once six modules could be plugged into the shell, the next thing in the way was a Pi that was screwed to the wall behind them. The shell with its plate was exported on the evening of 13 May and again as two separate parts the next afternoon. The shell was saved once more as BaseSmaller that evening, and the plate was exported for shorter screws the day after. The three exports of 14 May that still carry the clamp ring name are a small sign of the pace of that week. They were probably saved from a window that still had the old file name in it.

The Pi code was being written in the same days. `config.py` and the setup service were saved on 9 May, the calibration page on 16 May and the GPIO driver on 19 May, so the computer was going into the shell at the same time as its software went onto the computer.

| Feature | Fixed posts, 9 May | Sliding plate, 21 May |
|---|---|---|
| Posts | Four on the wall, 6 mm diameter, 5 mm long | Two on the plate, 6 mm diameter, 3 mm long |
| Screw hole | 1.8 mm, 6 mm deep | 2.8 mm, 6 mm deep, the same as the bolt hole of the clamp ring |
| Hole pattern | 58 by 23 mm | 58 mm apart, upper pair only |
| Height of the Pi centre above the rim | 15 mm | 10 mm |
| Plate | None | 77 by 28 by 2 mm with a 1 mm chamfer on the leading edge |
| Rails | None | Two blocks 5 mm wide with a lip of 3 by 2 mm and a cap 3 mm tall at the inner end |
| Running clearance | None | 0.4 mm |
| Click | None | A half cylinder of radius 1 mm in each rail, 26 mm in, that drops into a pocket of radius 1.2 mm in the side of the plate |

The hole pattern follows the mechanical drawing of the board. The source file carries the reasoning as a comment. The board is 65 by 30 mm and each hole sits 3.5 mm in from the edge, which gives 58 by 23 mm.

The plate is pushed in from the open rim of the shell toward the floor until its edge meets the cap of the rails. In that position the two half cylinders sit in their pockets. Because the plate leaves through the rim, the Pi can be taken out after the middle shell is lifted off, without touching the actuators.

## Motor driver clips
![[MooMooRedesign_28_bottom_casing_driver_clips.png]]
The same shell seen from the other side. The three clips for the TB6612FNG boards stand on the near wall, opposite the Pi

Each clip is a pocket 12.5 mm wide and 2 mm deep, 20 mm tall, with a wall of 3 mm and a lip of 3 mm across its top. A driver board slides into the pocket from the rim. The three clips stand 30 mm apart. Three boards with two channels each give the six channels that the Pi code expects. The width of the pocket was 13.5 mm in the file of 24 April and 12.5 mm from 9 May on.

![[MooMooRedesign_28_bottom_casing_plan.png]]
Plan view from above. The rails and the plate are at the bottom edge of the picture

## Other features on the floor
The shell of 9 May also gained a block of 35 by 35 by 10 mm with two slots of 4 mm in the middle of the floor, four small clips with a 3 mm slot at X equal to plus and minus 35 and 70 mm, and a slot of 20 by 3 by 20 mm through the front wall for a cable.

## Wiring that the plate has to carry
The Pi code lists the lines that run between the Pi and the rest of the shell.

| Lines | Count | GPIO numbers in `braille_gpio.py` |
|---|---|---|
| Motor direction, two per channel | 12 | 4 and 14, 17 and 15, 27 and 18, 22 and 23, 10 and 24, 9 and 25 |
| Push buttons, one per dot | 6 | 11, 5, 6, 13, 19, 16 |
| Standby of all three driver boards | 1 | 26 |

That is nineteen signal wires, plus power and ground. A Pi on a sliding plate is only removable if those wires can follow it or unplug.

```
include <BOSL2/std.scad>
$fa = 1;
$fs = 0.4;

// ============================================================
// PART SELECTION
//   "base"  -> casing + all attached components
//   "plate" -> just the slidable plate
//   "all"   -> both, assembled
// ============================================================
PART = "all";

// ============================================================
// CASING
// ============================================================
LENGTH         = 205;
WIDTH          = 140;
HEIGHT         = 40;
ROUNDING       = 10;
EDGE_THICKNESS = 3;

HOLE_SIZE = 8.45;
HOLE_H    = 35;
HOLE_Z    = 25;
HOLE_POS  = [
  [   0,  32.5, HOLE_Z ],
  [   0, -32.5, HOLE_Z ],
  [ -65,  32.5, HOLE_Z ],
  [ -65, -32.5, HOLE_Z ],
  [  65,  32.5, HOLE_Z ],
  [  65, -32.5, HOLE_Z ],
];

SUP_OUT_H    = 15;
SUP_OUT_SIZE = 10.2;
SUP_IN_H     = 15;
SUP_IN_SIZE  = 8.45;

BACK_HOLDER_POS    = [ 99.5, 0, 20 ];
BACK_HOLDER_RADIUS = 5;
BACK_HOLDER_HEIGHT = 10;

FRONT_HOLDER_POS    = [ -99.5, 0, 20 ];
FRONT_HOLDER_RADIUS = 3;
FRONT_HOLDER_HEIGHT = 6;

BACK_SUPPORT_POS = [ 97, 0, 20 ];
BACK_SUP_OUT_R   = 6;
BACK_SUP_IN_R    = 5;
BACK_SUP_H       = 8;

WALL_Y = WIDTH/2 - EDGE_THICKNESS;

// ============================================================
// RASPBERRY PI ZERO 2W MOUNTING (on the slider)
//   Screw hole diameter matches the clamp's bolt hole (BOLT_R*2 = 2.8)
// ============================================================
PI_POST_D           = 6;
PI_MOUNT_LENGTH     = 3;
PI_SCREW_HOLE_D     = 2.8;
PI_SCREW_HOLE_DEPTH = 6;

PI_HOLE_SPACING_X = 58;
PI_HOLE_SPACING_Y = 23;
PI_CENTER_Z       = 10;

// ============================================================
// SLIDER  (parametric — adjust any of the four edges independently)
// ============================================================
SLIDER_LEFT   = -38.5;   // left   edge X
SLIDER_RIGHT  =  38.5;   // right  edge X
SLIDER_BOTTOM =   0;     // bottom edge Z
SLIDER_TOP    =  28;     // top    edge Z
SLIDER_T      =   2;     // thickness (Y)
SLIDER_TOL    =   0.4;   // running clearance against the rails

SLIDER_W       = SLIDER_RIGHT - SLIDER_LEFT;
SLIDER_H       = SLIDER_TOP   - SLIDER_BOTTOM;
SLIDER_Y_BACK  = WALL_Y;
SLIDER_Y_FRONT = WALL_Y - SLIDER_T;

// Pi post positions (on the slider's front face)
PI_PLATE_POS = [
  [ -PI_HOLE_SPACING_X/2, SLIDER_Y_FRONT, PI_CENTER_Z + PI_HOLE_SPACING_Y/2 ],
  [  PI_HOLE_SPACING_X/2, SLIDER_Y_FRONT, PI_CENTER_Z + PI_HOLE_SPACING_Y/2 ],
];

// Rails (each side tracks the corresponding slider edge)
RAIL_BLOCK_W = 5;
RAIL_LIP_W   = 3;
RAIL_LIP_T   = 2;
RAIL_CAP_Z   = 3;

// Click cylinders (in the rail lips, mate with slider edges)
CLICK_CYL_R      = 1;
CLICK_CYL_L      = 3.5;
CLICK_CYL_Z      = 26;
CLICK_CYL_OFFSET = 0.2;   // distance from slider edge to cylinder centre

// ============================================================
// TB6612 HOLDERS
// ============================================================
TB_BOARD_WIDTH = 10.5;
TB_PCB_WIDTH   = 12.5;
TB_CLIP_HEIGHT = 20;
TB_CLIP_DEPTH  = 2;
TB_WALL_THICK  = 3;

TB_POSITIONS = [
  [ -30 - TB_BOARD_WIDTH/2, -WALL_Y, 8 ],
  [   0 - TB_BOARD_WIDTH/2, -WALL_Y, 8 ],
  [  30 - TB_BOARD_WIDTH/2, -WALL_Y, 8 ],
];

// ============================================================
// SNAP FIT
// ============================================================
CUBE_WIDTH  = 4;
CUBE_LENGTH = 8;
CUBE_HEIGHT = 15;
CUBE_Z      = -1;
CUBE_POSITIONS = [
  [[ -97.5, -30 ], [ 90, 0,   0 ]],
  [[ -97.5,  30 ], [ 90, 0,   0 ]],
  [[  97.5, -30 ], [ 90, 0, 180 ]],
  [[  97.5,  30 ], [ 90, 0, 180 ]],
  [[   50,   65 ], [ 90, 0, -90 ]],
  [[  -50,   65 ], [ 90, 0, -90 ]],
  [[   50,  -65 ], [ 90, 0, -90 ]],
  [[  -50,  -65 ], [ 90, 0, -90 ]],
];

TRIANGLE_HEIGHT = 10.5;
TRIANGLE_Z      = -2.3;
TRIANGLE_RADIUS = 2.7;
TRIANGLE_POSITIONS = [
  [[ -98.3, -30  ], [ 90, 0,   0 ]],
  [[ -98.3,  30  ], [ 90, 0,   0 ]],
  [[  98.3, -30  ], [ 90, 0, 180 ]],
  [[  98.3,  30  ], [ 90, 0, 180 ]],
  [[   50,   65.8], [ 90, 0, -90 ]],
  [[  -50,   65.8], [ 90, 0, -90 ]],
  [[   50,  -65.8], [ 90, 0,  90 ]],
  [[  -50,  -65.8], [ 90, 0,  90 ]],
];

DOT_R   = 1;
DOT_POS = [
  [ 99,  0, 25 ],
  [ 99,  0, 15 ],
  [ 99,  5, 20 ],
  [ 99, -5, 20 ],
];

// ============================================================
// MODULES — CASING
// ============================================================
module casing(length, width, height, rounding) {
  cuboid([ length, width, height ],
    anchor  = BOT,
    chamfer = rounding,
    edges   = [ TOP, FRONT+RIGHT, FRONT+LEFT, BACK+RIGHT, BACK+LEFT ],
    $fn = 128
  );
}

module cableCutout() {
  translate([ -100, 0, 0 ])
    cube([ 20, 3, 20 ], anchor=BOT);
}

// ============================================================
// MODULES — PIN HOLES + SUPPORTS
// ============================================================
module holes() {
  move_copies(HOLE_POS)
    cube([ HOLE_SIZE, HOLE_SIZE, HOLE_H ], anchor=BOT+CENTER);
}

module holeSupport() {
  move_copies(HOLE_POS)
    difference() {
      cube([ SUP_OUT_SIZE, SUP_OUT_SIZE, SUP_OUT_H ], anchor=BOT+CENTER);
      cube([ SUP_IN_SIZE,  SUP_IN_SIZE,  SUP_IN_H  ], anchor=BOT+CENTER);
    }
}

// ============================================================
// MODULES — SHAPE HOLDERS
// ============================================================
module backShapeHolderHole() {
  translate(BACK_HOLDER_POS)
    rotate([ 0, 90, 0 ])
      cylinder(h=BACK_HOLDER_HEIGHT, r=BACK_HOLDER_RADIUS, center=true, $fn=64);
}

module frontShapeHolderHole() {
  translate(FRONT_HOLDER_POS)
    rotate([ 0, 90, 0 ])
      cylinder(h=FRONT_HOLDER_HEIGHT, r=FRONT_HOLDER_RADIUS, center=true, $fn=64);
}

module backShapeHolderSupport() {
  translate(BACK_SUPPORT_POS)
    rotate([ 0, 90, 0 ])
      difference() {
        cylinder(h=BACK_SUP_H,     r=BACK_SUP_OUT_R,     center=true, $fn=64);
        cylinder(h=BACK_SUP_H + 2, r=BACK_SUP_IN_R, center=true, $fn=64);
      }
}

// ============================================================
// MODULES — RAILS + CLICK
// ============================================================
module piMountRail(side) {
  plate_edge_x = (side > 0) ? SLIDER_RIGHT : SLIDER_LEFT;
  wall_x_a = plate_edge_x + side * SLIDER_TOL;
  wall_x_b = plate_edge_x + side * (SLIDER_TOL + RAIL_BLOCK_W);
  lip_x_b  = plate_edge_x - side * RAIL_LIP_W;

  lip_front_y = WALL_Y - SLIDER_T - SLIDER_TOL - RAIL_LIP_T;
  lip_back_y  = WALL_Y - SLIDER_T - SLIDER_TOL;
  z_top       = SLIDER_TOP + RAIL_CAP_Z;

  translate([min(wall_x_a, wall_x_b), lip_front_y, SLIDER_BOTTOM])
    cube([ abs(wall_x_b - wall_x_a),
           WALL_Y - lip_front_y,
           z_top - SLIDER_BOTTOM ]);

  translate([min(wall_x_b, lip_x_b), lip_front_y, SLIDER_BOTTOM])
    cube([ abs(lip_x_b - wall_x_b),
           lip_back_y - lip_front_y,
           z_top - SLIDER_BOTTOM ]);

  translate([min(wall_x_a, lip_x_b), lip_back_y, SLIDER_TOP])
    cube([ abs(lip_x_b - wall_x_a),
           WALL_Y - lip_back_y,
           RAIL_CAP_Z ]);
}

module piMountRails() {
  piMountRail(-1);
  piMountRail( 1);
}

module clickCylinder(side) {
  edge_x = (side > 0) ? SLIDER_RIGHT : SLIDER_LEFT;
  translate([edge_x + side * CLICK_CYL_OFFSET, WALL_Y, CLICK_CYL_Z])
    rotate([90, 0, 0])
      cylinder(CLICK_CYL_L, CLICK_CYL_R, CLICK_CYL_R);
}

module clickCylinders() {
  clickCylinder(-1);
  clickCylinder( 1);
}

// Matching pockets cut into the slider sides
// (slightly oversized for running clearance)
CLICK_HOLE_R   = CLICK_CYL_R + 0.2;
CLICK_HOLE_L   = CLICK_CYL_L + 1;

module clickCylinderHole(side) {
  edge_x = (side > 0) ? SLIDER_RIGHT : SLIDER_LEFT;
  // Centred at the slider edge so the pocket is a half-cylinder
  // cut into the side face of the slider.
  translate([edge_x, WALL_Y, CLICK_CYL_Z])
    rotate([90, 0, 0])
      cylinder(CLICK_HOLE_L, CLICK_HOLE_R, CLICK_HOLE_R);
}

module clickCylinderHoles() {
  clickCylinderHole(-1);
  clickCylinderHole( 1);
}

// ============================================================
// MODULES — SLIDER
// ============================================================
module slider() {
  difference() {
    union() {
      // Slider body
      translate([(SLIDER_LEFT + SLIDER_RIGHT)/2, SLIDER_Y_FRONT, SLIDER_BOTTOM])
        cuboid([SLIDER_W, SLIDER_T, SLIDER_H],chamfer=1,edges=TOP, anchor=BOT+FRONT);
      // Pi mounting posts (extending forward from the slider's front face)
      move_copies(PI_PLATE_POS)
        rotate([90, 0, 0])
          cylinder(h=PI_MOUNT_LENGTH, d=PI_POST_D, $fn=32);
    }
    // Pi screw holes
    move_copies(PI_PLATE_POS)
      translate([0, 0.1, 0])
        rotate([90, 0, 0])
          cylinder(h=PI_SCREW_HOLE_DEPTH, d=PI_SCREW_HOLE_D, $fn=32);
    // Click cylinder pockets on the slider sides
    clickCylinderHoles();
  }
}

// ============================================================
// MODULES — TB6612 HOLDERS
// ============================================================
module tb6612ClipHolder() {
  side_offset = (TB_BOARD_WIDTH - TB_PCB_WIDTH) / 2;
  difference() {
    union() {
      translate([ side_offset, 0, 0 ])
        cube([ TB_PCB_WIDTH, TB_CLIP_DEPTH + TB_WALL_THICK, TB_CLIP_HEIGHT ]);
      translate([ side_offset, 0, TB_CLIP_HEIGHT ])
        cube([ TB_PCB_WIDTH, TB_CLIP_DEPTH + TB_WALL_THICK, TB_WALL_THICK ]);
    }
    translate([ side_offset, 1, 0 ])
      cube([ TB_PCB_WIDTH, TB_CLIP_DEPTH, TB_CLIP_HEIGHT + 1 ]);
  }
}

module tb6612Holders() {
  move_copies(TB_POSITIONS)
    tb6612ClipHolder();
}

// ============================================================
// MODULES — SNAP FIT
// ============================================================
module snapCubesWithHoles() {
  for (pos = CUBE_POSITIONS)
    translate([ pos[0][0], pos[0][1], CUBE_Z ])
      rotate(pos[1])
        cube([ CUBE_WIDTH, CUBE_LENGTH, CUBE_HEIGHT ], anchor=CENTER);
}

module triangleMarkers() {
  for (pos = TRIANGLE_POSITIONS)
    translate([ pos[0][0], pos[0][1], TRIANGLE_Z ])
      rotate(pos[1])
        cylinder(TRIANGLE_HEIGHT, TRIANGLE_RADIUS, TRIANGLE_RADIUS, anchor=CENTER, $fn=3);
}

// ============================================================
// MODULES — GRIP DOTS
// ============================================================
module gripDots() {
  move_copies(DOT_POS)
    sphere(r=DOT_R);
}

// ============================================================
// MODULES — TOP MOUNT FEATURES
// ============================================================
module topMountBlock() {
  difference() {
    translate([0,0,32])
      cube([35,35,10], center=true);
    translate([-10,0,35])
      cube([4,40,4], center=true);
    translate([10,0,35])
      cube([4,40,4], center=true);
  }
}

module topClip(x) {
  translate([x, 0, 35.5])
    difference() {
      cube([5,5,5], center=true);
      cube([3,8,3], center=true);
    }
}

module topClips() {
  topClip(-70);
  topClip( 70);
  topClip( 35);
  topClip(-35);
}

// ============================================================
// BASE  (casing with every attached component)
// ============================================================
module base() {
  // Hollow casing
  difference() {
    casing(LENGTH, WIDTH, HEIGHT, ROUNDING);
    casing(
      LENGTH - EDGE_THICKNESS*2,
      WIDTH  - EDGE_THICKNESS*2,
      HEIGHT - EDGE_THICKNESS,
      ROUNDING - EDGE_THICKNESS/2
    );
    holes();
    backShapeHolderHole();
    frontShapeHolderHole();
    cableCutout();
  }

  // Snap fit
  difference() {
    snapCubesWithHoles();
    triangleMarkers();
  }

  // Additive features
  gripDots();
  holeSupport();
  backShapeHolderSupport();
  piMountRails();
  clickCylinders();
  tb6612Holders();
  topMountBlock();
  topClips();
}

// ============================================================
// ASSEMBLY
// ============================================================
if (PART == "base") {
  base();
}
else if (PART == "plate") {
  slider();
}
else {
  //base();
  slider();
}
```

# Critical Reflection
## A plate that slides against posts on the wall
**Pros**
- The Pi and its wiring can leave the shell as one unit, which continues the module idea of step 24 at the scale of the computer
- The screws are driven from outside the shell while the plate is in the hand, not at the bottom of a 40 mm deep box
- The shell prints the same way as before. Rails, clips and sockets all rise straight from the closed face, so the slicer project needs supports only where they were placed by hand
- The plate is a separate export, so a change of computer means printing one part of 77 by 28 mm and not the whole shell

**Cons**
- The Pi now hangs on two screws where it used to sit on four
- A running clearance of 0.4 mm is a small number for a plate 77 mm long. The shell was exported again as BaseSmaller on the first evening, which suggests the first rails did not let the plate run freely
- The board outline in the images shows that the Pi reaches about 5 mm past the rim of the bottom shell, into the space of the middle shell. That is fine as long as nothing in the middle shell sits against that wall

## The boards stand upright
Both the Pi and the driver boards stand on edge against the walls. That keeps the floor free for six sockets that cannot move, because their positions are fixed by the top shell (step 21). It also puts the Pi on one long wall and the drivers on the other, 134 mm apart, with the motors between them. Every direction line crosses the row of actuators.
