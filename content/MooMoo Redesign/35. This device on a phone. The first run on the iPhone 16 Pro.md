# Goals
- Run Moo's listening, stories and voice in the browser of a phone, with no computer and no cloud
- Find out what a phone can carry, since the models were so far only loaded in Chrome on a Mac
- Let each device choose its own models

# Description
Step 32 gave the Moo a choice named This device, in which the browser of the phone or laptop in the parent's hand does the AI work. It had been tried once, in Chrome on the Mac. On 21 November 2026 the maker tried it on his iPhone 16 Pro, the lower end device he had named as the next thing to test. A check on 20 November had shown that the phone's browser has what the method needs.

The first try did not get as far as a story. When the maker tapped This device, the website crashed. He then asked whether models and voices can be chosen by preference on phones and tablets as they can on the computer.

# How it works
## What the log showed
A phone browser that runs out of memory does not show an error. It closes the page and opens it again. The Moo website remembered that this device had been chosen, so every fresh page began to load the models once more, and the page closed once more. The setting for the size of the models was shared by all devices and stood on Better, the 3 billion parameter story model of about 2.5 GB that had been chosen for the Mac. The maker changed it on the phone during these two minutes, and it ended on Instant, so smaller models may have been tried as well. Which model the phone was loading when it closed could not be seen from the log.

## Three changes
| Change | What it does |
|---|---|
| A note before each model | Before the story model, the listening model and the voice are loaded, the page writes a note on the device and tells the Moo, which logs it. When a model has loaded, the note is removed. A note that is still there on the next page is the model that closed the browser |
| No loading by itself after a crash | With such a note present the page does not start loading again. The AI engine screen says which model closed the browser and asks for smaller ones |
| Models chosen on the device | The story model and the listening model of This device can be chosen on the screen and are remembered on that device only, so a phone and a computer each keep their own. Eight story models from 360 million to 3 billion parameters and two sizes of Whisper are offered. On an iPhone or iPad the smaller Whisper is the default |

At that point the voice on a lent device was still Kokoro, with its ten voices.

## The second try
The maker could not reach the secure address in Chrome on the phone at first and opened the plain one, from which the page moves to the secure address by itself. This time the trace worked. The screen told him that the browser had closed while loading the listening model, and the Pi's log showed the same order twice: the story model Qwen 2.5 0.5B, then Whisper tiny, then nothing. The smallest listening model there is had closed the page, so its size was not the cause. The story model was already on the phone's graphics chip, and Whisper was being loaded onto the same chip in its full 32 bit form. On iPhones and iPads Whisper now runs on the processor in its 8 bit form, the way the Kokoro voice already does.

## The third try
With Whisper on the processor the phone got one model further. The log shows the story model, then Whisper, which loaded in two seconds, then the Kokoro voice, and there the page closed again. Two of the three models now fit, and the third does not. The story model Qwen 2.5 0.5B alone asks for close to 1 GB on the graphics chip, and a page in a phone browser gets far less memory than the phone has. Either the story model has to be smaller still, or the voice on a phone has to be a much lighter one than Kokoro.

## The fourth and fifth try
The maker's choice of a smaller story model had not reached the phone on the fourth try, and the page closed at the Kokoro voice once more. iPhones and iPads were then given the smallest story model, SmolLM2 with 360 million parameters, as their default, and half the usual working memory for it. On the fifth try that model was downloaded and loaded, Whisper loaded, and the page closed at the Kokoro voice again. The maker's own reading was that the story model loads fine and the listening or the voice model is the problem. The log agreed and named the voice. With the smallest story model in place the total could no longer be the reason. Kokoro itself does not fit into a page on this phone.

## A voice made for phone browsers
The voice on a phone is now Kitten, through a library named kitten-tts-webgpu that was written for phone browsers. It runs on the graphics chip without the part that Kokoro needs for turning text into sounds, which its authors say hangs on iPhones, and it states a peak of about 130 MB on a phone. It carries the first Kitten in three sizes of 24, 41 and 78 MB with the eight voices of that model. The maker had found that Kitten's Bruno quite good and its Bella poor on 20 November, so Bruno is the default. In a browser on the Mac the 41 MB size made a line of the letter game, 4.3 seconds of speech, in 0.9 seconds, and the 24 MB size made it in 0.6 seconds. A part longer than a few sentences is spoken sentence by sentence and joined.

Each device now chooses four things for itself: the story model, the listening model, the voice model and the voice. On iPhone and iPad the defaults are SmolLM2 360M, Whisper tiny on the processor and Kitten micro with Bruno. On a computer they stay Llama 3.2, Whisper base and Kokoro. Commit `2265f47`.

## The first run that worked
At 16:17 the iPhone loaded all three models in nine seconds, the story model SmolLM2 360M, Whisper tiny and the Kitten voice, and reported itself to the Moo as a lent device. It then did its first two jobs, a line of story and its voice, and the Moo played them. This was the first time a phone did the whole AI work for the Moo.

The maker's verdict on the result was short. The voice was bad, and the text was cut off again. To look at the cut, the same Kitten voice was run in a browser on the Mac, four lines in two sizes, and each recording was written out by speech recognition. Every word came back, also the last ones, so the sound that leaves the browser is whole and the cut is not explained by it. The recordings showed something else. Given several sentences at once, this Kitten runs them together with no pause, and "He was scared of the dark. One night a firefly came" was heard as "scared of the dark one, night, a firefly came". Each sentence is now spoken on its own with a short pause after it. The larger Kitten of 78 MB became the default on phones, and Kokoro on the graphics chip was added as a voice to try, since it was Kokoro on the processor that had closed the page. Commit `56023f9`.

The maker then asked whether the phone's chip can be used as well as the Mac's, and whether the Mac would fail in the same way without its models loaded beforehand. It would not. The models are not loaded beforehand on the Mac either, and the browser on the Mac loaded a 3 billion parameter story model, Whisper and Kokoro together on 20 November. The difference is how much memory a page is given. A phone browser closes a page at a small part of the phone's memory, while a program installed on the phone may use most of it and can reach the chip's own AI parts. An app on the phone, as Moo Local is a program on the Mac, would be the way to use the chip fully. That is a project of its own and was not started.

The maker tried Kokoro on the graphics chip on the phone, and the page ran out of memory again. Kokoro does not fit into a page on this phone in either form. He then asked for an app for the iPhone to be tried, written in React Native.

## What stands in the way of an app, and one more try in the browser
An app for the iPhone can only be built with Xcode, and the maker's Mac has only the small command line tools, no Xcode, no CocoaPods and no identity for signing an app. These need the maker's Apple ID and password, so the app waits for him to install them. A library was found that would carry all three jobs inside an app, with story models, Whisper and Kokoro prepared for the phone's own hardware, and the app could speak to the Moo exactly as the browser page does, so nothing on the Moo would have to change.

The maker asked whether the memory of a phone browser can be raised. It cannot. The limit belongs to the system, every browser on the iPhone uses the same engine, and neither the user nor a website can change it. What a page can do is need less at one moment. The three models were all kept loaded at once. In a new mode only the model that the next job needs stays loaded, and the others are loaded again from the phone's storage when their turn comes. At the start each model is fetched once, so that the later changes need no download. The mode is the default on iPhone and iPad and a choice on every device. It may let the phone carry a larger story model, or Kokoro, since each then has the page to itself, at the price of a wait at every change of model. Commit `542734a`. It was built and installed but could not be tried before the maker's phone ran it.

# Checked on the device, 21 November 2026
The report from a device reached the Pi's log in a test from the Pi itself. The new screen was built and installed. On the iPhone the note and the message after a crash worked as meant on the second try. The iPhone loaded all three models and worked for the Moo at 16:17. The later changes to the voice were tried in a browser on the Mac only.

# Critical Reflection
## A phone as the whole AI
**Pros**
- Nothing to install and nothing to pay. A parent opens a website
- The crash now leaves a trace, on the device and in the Moo's log, where before there was none
- A phone is no longer handed the models that were chosen for a computer

**Cons**
- It took six tries. This device had only ever been loaded on a Mac, and a phone browser gives a page far less memory than the device has
- What fits on the phone is the smallest of everything. The maker found the voice bad, and the story model is the weakest one of step 32's kind
- The cut off text that the maker heard is not explained
- Three models have to fit at the same time, for the story, the listening and the voice. On a phone that may only be possible with the smallest of each, and step 32 measured how plain the smallest story models are
- The eight story models offered are named from the library's list and have not each been loaded once. A name the library does not know gives an error on the screen
- The phone has to stay awake with the page open for as long as the child plays
