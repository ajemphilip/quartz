# Goals
- Bring every printed part of one braille dot together as one set that can be printed, assembled and replaced as a unit
- Shorten the button stack so that it fits under the top shell
- Guide the press from the cap to the button so that it also works when the cap is pressed off centre
- Print the braille cap in a soft material that grips the post by itself

# Description
By 6 May 2026 every part of the actuator had its own file in the folder finalMooMoo. This step follows those parts over the next two weeks until they sit together on one print plate. One braille dot is now made of twelve printed parts, one motor, one push button and one bolt.

From the bottom up the parts are the shaft body with its detachable half and clamp ring (step 24), the lead screw and the elevator nut (step 26), the lifter, the transfer cube, the two halves of the split button cylinder, the piston, the presser casing and the braille cap. Nine of them, all in PETG, were saved together as `WholeShaft.3mf` late on 12 May. The screw and the nut are printed separately at a finer layer height, and the cap is printed in TPU.

## How the press reaches the button
The split cylinder closes around the push button and holds it by its thread. It has an outside radius of 8.5 mm and sits inside the transfer cylinder and the presser casing, which both have a bore of 8.6 mm. The piston lies on top of the button. Its disc has a radius of 8.3 mm, so it has 0.3 mm of room all around in the same bore, and its post of radius 2.7 mm leaves through the top tube of the presser casing, which has a bore of 3 mm. The braille cap is pushed onto the end of that post.

The piston also carries two thin cross pins of radius 0.75 mm and length 20 mm. They run in the cross slots, 2 mm wide, that are cut through the wall of the presser casing and the transfer cylinder. The pins stop the piston from turning and from tipping. The reflection of [[6. New Motor testing and Pin Reconstruction]] recorded that the button only clicked when the cap was pressed in its centre. As modelled, a press near the edge of the 40 mm cap is now carried down by a guided piston and not by the cap alone.

| Part | Main values |
|---|---|
| Transfer cube | Cube 11.2 by 11.2 by 12 mm, cylinder of radius 10.6 and 8.6 mm and 10 mm height, cross slots 2 mm wide, two beams of 16 by 3 by 3 mm, two slanted cable holes of radius 3 mm |
| Split button cylinder | Radius 8.5 and 6.5 mm, 18 mm tall, hollowed M14 thin nut, torus ring, cut 0.35 mm wide, two three sided pins of radius 1.15 mm in holes of radius 1.4 mm, one 3 mm cube key on each side |
| Piston | Disc of radius 8.3 mm and 2 mm height, post of radius 2.7 mm and 16 mm length, cross pins of radius 0.75 mm |
| Presser casing | Radius 10.6 and 8.6 mm, 14.5 mm tall with a 2 mm roof, top tube of radius 5 and 3 mm and 7.5 mm height, cross slots 2 by 31 by 12.5 mm, four grip dimples of radius 0.7 mm |
| Braille cap | Cup of 40 mm outside and 36 mm inside diameter, 30 mm tall with a 5 mm roof. Post of 9.6 mm diameter and 11 mm length under the roof. Recess of 5.6 mm diameter and 6.5 mm depth with four bumps of radius 0.7 mm |

## The braille cap in TPU
![[MooMooRedesign_27_braille_cap_sections.png]]
Section through the two cap files. 1 `topBraille.scad` of 6 May with the long post, 2 `topBraillelowerAttachement.scad` of 11 May with the short post and the piston placed underneath it

The recess in the cap is 5.6 mm wide and the piston post is 5.4 mm wide. The four bumps inside the recess stand 3 mm from the axis with a radius of 0.7 mm, so they reach 0.5 mm into the recess and are about 0.4 mm tighter than the post. The post has no groove for them. The cap therefore holds by squeezing, which only works if the cap can give. The slicer project saved one minute after the shorter piston answers that. The cap is printed in Ultrafuse TPU 64D.

A soft cap also matters for the hand. It is the only part of the device that a reader touches while reading, and at 40 mm across it is a braille dot enlarged in the way that [[2. Pin Block and Movement Solutions. Device exterior pin holder casing preparation]] first proposed.

## Code
### Transfer cube
```
include <BOSL2/std.scad>
include <BOSL2/screws.scad>

$fa = 1;
$fs = 0.1;

// ======================================
// Parameters
// ======================================
CYLINDER_OUTER_RADIUS = 10.6;
CYLINDER_INNER_RADIUS = 8.6;
CYLINDER_HEIGHT = 10;
TRANSFER_CUBE_SIZE = [11.2, 11.2, 12];
CABLE_HOLE_RADIUS = 3;
CABLE_HOLE_LENGTH = 11;

GRIP_DOT_RADIUS = 0.7;
GRIP_DOT_DISTANCE = 8.6;
GRIP_DOT_TOP_Z = -1;

SLOT_WIDTH = 2;
SLOT_LENGTH = 30;
SLOT_HEIGHT = 12;

// ======================================
// Cylinder bottom
// ======================================
module cylinderBottom() {
    translate([0, 0, 0])
    difference() {
        cylinder(CYLINDER_HEIGHT, CYLINDER_OUTER_RADIUS, CYLINDER_OUTER_RADIUS, center=true);
        cylinder(CYLINDER_HEIGHT, CYLINDER_INNER_RADIUS, CYLINDER_INNER_RADIUS, center=true);
    }
}

// ======================================
// Transfer cube
// ======================================
module transferCube() {
    translate([0, 0, -10])
    cube(TRANSFER_CUBE_SIZE, center=true);
}

// ======================================
// Cable holes
// ======================================
module cableHoles() {
    rotate([45, 0, 90])
    translate([0, 2, -6])
    cylinder(CABLE_HOLE_LENGTH, CABLE_HOLE_RADIUS, CABLE_HOLE_RADIUS, center=true);
    
    rotate([45, 0, -90])
    translate([0, 2, -6])
    cylinder(CABLE_HOLE_LENGTH, CABLE_HOLE_RADIUS, CABLE_HOLE_RADIUS, center=true);
}

// ======================================
// Transfer cube with holes
// ======================================
module transferCubeWithHoles() {
    difference() {
        transferCube();
        cableHoles();
    }
}

module gripDots() {
    translate([0, GRIP_DOT_DISTANCE, GRIP_DOT_TOP_Z]) 
        sphere(r=GRIP_DOT_RADIUS);
    
    translate([0, -GRIP_DOT_DISTANCE, GRIP_DOT_TOP_Z]) 
        sphere(r=GRIP_DOT_RADIUS);
    
    translate([GRIP_DOT_DISTANCE, 0, GRIP_DOT_TOP_Z]) 
        sphere(r=GRIP_DOT_RADIUS);
    
    translate([-GRIP_DOT_DISTANCE, 0, GRIP_DOT_TOP_Z]) 
        sphere(r=GRIP_DOT_RADIUS);
}

module crossSlots() {
    ANGLES = [0, 90];
    for (angle = ANGLES) {
        rotate([0, 0, angle])
        cube([SLOT_LENGTH, SLOT_WIDTH, SLOT_HEIGHT], center=true);
    }
}

// ======================================
// Assembly
// ======================================
module assembly() {
    transferCubeWithHoles();
    difference() {
    cylinderBottom();
      crossSlots();
      rotate([0,0,45])
      gripDots();
     }
     
}

translate([0,5,-5])
cube([16,3,3],center=true);

translate([0,-5,-5])
cube([16,3,3],center=true);

assembly();
```

### Split button cylinder
```
include <BOSL2/std.scad>
include <BOSL2/screws.scad>

$fa = 1;
$fs = 0.1;

// ======================================
// Parameters
// ======================================

// Middle trim (removes this much from the empty band, raising the bottom)
WAIST_TRIM = 8;

CYLINDER_OUTER_RADIUS = 8.5;
CYLINDER_INNER_RADIUS = 6.5;
CYLINDER_HEIGHT = 26 - WAIST_TRIM;
CYLINDER_Z = 11 + WAIST_TRIM/2;     // top stays at z=24, bottom rises to z=-2+WAIST_TRIM

// Derived: new cylinder bottom Z (where split pins and splitter cubes start)
PIN_Z = CYLINDER_Z - CYLINDER_HEIGHT/2;

CABLE_HOLE_RADIUS = 3;
CABLE_HOLE_LENGTH = 11;
CABLE_HOLE_Z_SHIFT = WAIST_TRIM;

GRIP_DOT_RADIUS = 0.7;
GRIP_DOT_DISTANCE = 8.5;
GRIP_DOT_BOTTOM_Z = WAIST_TRIM;
GRIP_DOT_TOP_Z = 21.5;

TORUS_MAJOR_DIAMETER = 17.5;
TORUS_OUTER_DIAMETER = 18.5;
TORUS_Z = 18;

NUT_Z = 17;
NUT_HOLLOW_Z = 21.5;
NUT_HOLLOW_OUTER_RADIUS = 14;
NUT_HOLLOW_INNER_RADIUS = 8;
NUT_HOLLOW_HEIGHT = 10;

// ======================================
// Cylinder top
// ======================================

module cylinderTop() {
    translate([0, 0, CYLINDER_Z])
        difference() {
            cylinder(CYLINDER_HEIGHT, CYLINDER_OUTER_RADIUS, CYLINDER_OUTER_RADIUS, center=true);
            cylinder(CYLINDER_HEIGHT + 2, CYLINDER_INNER_RADIUS, CYLINDER_INNER_RADIUS, center=true);
        }
}

// ======================================
// Cable holes
// ======================================

module cableHoles() {
    translate([0, 0, CABLE_HOLE_Z_SHIFT]) {
        rotate([45, 0, 90])
            translate([0, 2, -6])
                cylinder(CABLE_HOLE_LENGTH, CABLE_HOLE_RADIUS, CABLE_HOLE_RADIUS, center=true);

        rotate([45, 0, -90])
            translate([0, 2, -6])
                cylinder(CABLE_HOLE_LENGTH, CABLE_HOLE_RADIUS, CABLE_HOLE_RADIUS, center=true);
    }
}

// ======================================
// Grip dots
// ======================================

module gripDots() {
    translate([0, GRIP_DOT_DISTANCE, 0])
        sphere(r=GRIP_DOT_RADIUS);

    translate([0, -GRIP_DOT_DISTANCE, 0])
        sphere(r=GRIP_DOT_RADIUS);

    translate([GRIP_DOT_DISTANCE, 0, 0])
        sphere(r=GRIP_DOT_RADIUS);

    translate([-GRIP_DOT_DISTANCE, 0, 0])
        sphere(r=GRIP_DOT_RADIUS);
}

module gripDotsBottom() {
    translate([0, 0, GRIP_DOT_BOTTOM_Z])
        gripDots();
}

module gripDotsTop() {
    translate([0, 0, GRIP_DOT_TOP_Z])
        gripDots();
}

// ======================================
// Torus ring
// ======================================

module torusRing() {
    translate([0, 0, TORUS_Z])
        torus(d_maj=TORUS_MAJOR_DIAMETER, od=TORUS_OUTER_DIAMETER);
}

// ======================================
// Hollowed nut
// ======================================

module hollowedNut() {
    difference() {
        translate([0, 0, NUT_Z])
            nut("M14", thickness="thin");

        translate([0, 0, NUT_HOLLOW_Z])
            difference() {
                cylinder(NUT_HOLLOW_HEIGHT, NUT_HOLLOW_OUTER_RADIUS, NUT_HOLLOW_OUTER_RADIUS, center=true);
                cylinder(NUT_HOLLOW_HEIGHT, NUT_HOLLOW_INNER_RADIUS, NUT_HOLLOW_INNER_RADIUS, center=true);
            }
    }
}

// ======================================
// Top with holes
// ======================================

module topWithHoles() {
    difference() {
        cylinderTop();
        cableHoles();
    }
}

// ======================================
// Assembly
// ======================================

module assembly() {
    topWithHoles();
    rotate([0, 0, 45])
        gripDotsBottom();
    rotate([0, 0, 45])
        gripDotsTop();
    torusRing();
    hollowedNut();
}

module LeftPart() {
    difference() {
        assembly();
        translate([0, 7.5, PIN_Z])
            cylinder(CYLINDER_HEIGHT, 1.4, 1.4, anchor=BOT+CENTER, $fn=3);
        translate([0, -7.5, PIN_Z])
            cylinder(CYLINDER_HEIGHT, 1.4, 1.4, anchor=BOT+CENTER, $fn=3);
        translate([-0.5, 0, PIN_Z])
            cube([0.35, 50, 50], anchor=BOT+CENTER);
        translate([-0.69, 0, PIN_Z])
            cube([50, 50, 50], anchor=BOT+LEFT);
    }
    translate([-0.2, 7.46, PIN_Z])
        cylinder(CYLINDER_HEIGHT, 1.15, 1.15, anchor=BOT+CENTER, $fn=3);
    translate([-0.2, -7.46, PIN_Z])
        cylinder(CYLINDER_HEIGHT, 1.15, 1.15, anchor=BOT+CENTER, $fn=3);
    intersectionLeft();
    intersectionRight();
}

module intersectionLeft() {
    intersection() {
        translate([0.8, -7.5, CYLINDER_Z])
            cube([3, 3, 3], anchor=CENTER);
        assembly();
    }
}

module intersectionRight() {
    intersection() {
        translate([0.8, 7.5, CYLINDER_Z])
            cube([3, 3, 3], anchor=CENTER);
        assembly();
    }
}

module rightPart() {
    difference() {
        assembly();
        translate([0, 7.5, PIN_Z])
            cylinder(CYLINDER_HEIGHT, 1.4, 1.4, anchor=BOT+CENTER, $fn=3);
        translate([0, -7.5, PIN_Z])
            cylinder(CYLINDER_HEIGHT, 1.4, 1.4, anchor=BOT+CENTER, $fn=3);
        translate([-0.5, 0, PIN_Z])
            cube([0.35, 50, 50], anchor=BOT+CENTER);
        translate([-0.65, 0, PIN_Z])
            rotate([0, 0, 180])
                cube([50, 50, 50], anchor=BOT+LEFT);
        translate([1.3, -7.5, CYLINDER_Z])
            cube([3.5, 3.5, 3.5], anchor=CENTER);
        translate([1.3, 7.5, CYLINDER_Z])
            cube([3.5, 3.5, 3.5], anchor=CENTER);
    }
}

LeftPart();
```

### Presser casing
```
include <BOSL2/std.scad>

$fa = 1;
$fs = 0.1;

// Shell
CYLINDER_OUTER_RADIUS = 10.6;
CYLINDER_INNER_RADIUS = 8.6;
CYLINDER_HEIGHT = 14.5;
INNER_OFFSET_Z = -2;
CENTER_HOLE_RADIUS = 5;

// Top tube
TOP_TUBE_OUTER_R = 5;
TOP_TUBE_INNER_R = 3;
TOP_TUBE_HEIGHT = 7.5;
TOP_TUBE_Z = 9;

// Cross slots
SLOT_WIDTH = 2;
SLOT_LENGTH = 31;
SLOT_HEIGHT = 12.5;
SLOT_Z = -1;

// Grip dimples
GRIP_DOT_RADIUS = 0.7;
GRIP_DOT_DISTANCE = 8.6;
GRIP_DOT_Z = -4.5;

module casingShell() {
    difference() {
        cylinder(CYLINDER_HEIGHT, CYLINDER_OUTER_RADIUS, CYLINDER_OUTER_RADIUS, center=true);
        translate([0, 0, INNER_OFFSET_Z])
            cylinder(CYLINDER_HEIGHT, CYLINDER_INNER_RADIUS, CYLINDER_INNER_RADIUS, center=true);
        cylinder(20, CENTER_HOLE_RADIUS, CENTER_HOLE_RADIUS, center=true);
    }
}

module topTube() {
    translate([0, 0, TOP_TUBE_Z])
        difference() {
            cylinder(TOP_TUBE_HEIGHT, TOP_TUBE_OUTER_R, TOP_TUBE_OUTER_R, center=true);
            cylinder(TOP_TUBE_HEIGHT, TOP_TUBE_INNER_R, TOP_TUBE_INNER_R, center=true);
        }
}

module crossSlots() {
    zrot_copies([0, 90])
        cube([SLOT_LENGTH, SLOT_WIDTH, SLOT_HEIGHT], center=true);
}

module gripDots() {
    zrot_copies(n=4)
        translate([GRIP_DOT_DISTANCE, 0, GRIP_DOT_Z])
            sphere(r=GRIP_DOT_RADIUS);
}

module casing() {
    difference() {
        union() {
            casingShell();
            topTube();
        }
        translate([0, 0, SLOT_Z])
            crossSlots();
        rotate([0, 0, 45])
            gripDots();
    }
}
```

### Braille cap
```
include <BOSL2/std.scad>

$fa = 0.5;
$fs = 0.01;

// ======================================
// Parameters
// ======================================

// Outer hollow cylinder (cup walls)
OUTER_OD = 40;
OUTER_ID = 36;
OUTER_HEIGHT = 30;

// Solid top disk (closes the cup)
TOP_DISK_HEIGHT = 5;
TOP_DISK_OD = 40;
TOP_DISK_Z = 30;

// Inner cylinder (boss hanging from the top)
INNER_OD = 9.6;
INNER_Z = 19;
INNER_HEIGHT = 11;

// Small recess inside the inner cylinder
RECESS_D = 5.6;
RECESS_HEIGHT = 6.5;

// Braille bumps on the recess wall
BUMP_RADIUS = 0.7;
BUMP_DISTANCE = 3.0;
BUMP_Z = 20;

// ======================================
// Modules
// ======================================

module outerCylinder() {
    difference() {
        cylinder(h=OUTER_HEIGHT, d=OUTER_OD);
        translate([0, 0, -0.01])
            cylinder(h=OUTER_HEIGHT + 0.02, d=OUTER_ID);
    }
}

module topDisk() {
    translate([0, 0, TOP_DISK_Z])
        cylinder(h=TOP_DISK_HEIGHT, d=TOP_DISK_OD);
}

module innerCylinder() {
    difference() {
        translate([0, 0, INNER_Z])
            cylinder(h=INNER_HEIGHT, d=INNER_OD);
        translate([0, 0, INNER_Z - 0.01])
            cylinder(h=RECESS_HEIGHT + 0.01, d=RECESS_D);
    }
}

module brailleBumps() {
    zrot_copies(n=4)
        translate([BUMP_DISTANCE, 0, BUMP_Z])
            sphere(r=BUMP_RADIUS);
}

// ======================================
// Assembly
// ======================================

module brailleCap() {
    outerCylinder();
    topDisk();
    innerCylinder();
    brailleBumps();
}

brailleCap();
```

# Critical Reflection
## Twelve printed parts for one dot
**Pros**
- Every part that can wear or break is small, and the file that makes it is short. The piston is 38 lines of OpenSCAD
- The set prints on one plate at 0.15 mm, so a spare module is one print job and not a project
- The three parts that need different treatment get it. The thread and the nut are printed at 0.10 mm, the cap is printed soft, everything else is printed fast
- The module repeats six times without any change, which is what makes the device repairable by someone who did not design it

**Cons**
- Twelve parts per dot is seventy two parts for one braille cell, before motors, buttons and bolts
- Clearances of 0.1 to 0.3 mm between nested cylinders are at the edge of what a 0.4 mm nozzle holds from print to print. The 0.5 mm adjustment of the presser casing on 16 May came after the full set was printed
- The stack was shortened by about 9 mm in three places in one night, so its height had been decided by eye before it was checked against the shells
